The platform helps individual investors and financial data users follow reported insider transactions through structured data extraction, validation workflows, and concise email and social updates.

## The Problem

SEC Form 4 filings provide public disclosures of transactions involving company insiders. However, the volume and technical structure of these filings can make consistent monitoring difficult for individual investors and other users of financial information.

AltData US is designed to reduce this information-processing burden.

It identifies relevant filings, extracts structured transaction information, applies validation rules, and presents selected events in concise, accessible briefings.

The objective is straightforward: make public regulatory disclosures easier to monitor without turning factual reporting into speculative investment commentary.

## Technical Architecture

AltData US is developed around four principles: data traceability, deterministic processing, controlled publication, and lean infrastructure.

### 1. SEC Data Ingestion

- Retrieves publicly available SEC EDGAR filing data.
- Processes relevant Form 4 disclosures.
- Uses local caching and controlled request handling to avoid unnecessary repeated retrievals.
- Retains source references to support subsequent verification.

### 2. Data Processing and Cross-Validation

Financial figures are extracted through code-based processing rather than generated by a language model.

The data validation design includes:

- **Independent Parser Cross-Validation:** The same locally stored source document can be processed through independent XML parsing implementations (e.g., `xml.etree.ElementTree` and `lxml.etree`).
- **Field-Level Comparison:** Extracted fields, including transaction dates, share quantities, prices, and transaction codes, are compared before publication.
- **Deterministic Validation:** Rule-based checks identify missing, inconsistent, or structurally unexpected values.
- **Fail-Closed Handling:** Records that fail validation are withheld from automated publication pending review.

LLMs are not treated as the source of truth for financial figures or transaction classifications. Reported data must remain traceable to the underlying SEC disclosure.

### 3. Human-in-the-Loop Approval

Automated processing does not mean unrestricted publication. 

Before distribution, generated briefing content passes through a human review and approval workflow. 

The publishing process is designed so that content requiring approval is not distributed until an authorized operator has reviewed it.

## Security and Operational Principles

AltData US follows a lightweight security approach appropriate for an independently operated SaaS platform. Key areas of focus include:

- Domain-based email authentication and sender protection.
- Secure, signed tokens for supported unsubscribe workflows.
- Server-side validation and rate controls for sensitive endpoints.
- Risk controls designed to reduce automated trial and referral abuse.
- Separation of application configuration and sensitive credentials from public-facing content.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is AltData US?
AltData US is a financial data monitoring platform focused on publicly available SEC insider transaction disclosures, particularly Form 4 filings.

### What data does AltData US monitor?
The platform focuses on SEC Form 4 disclosures, which report certain transactions and holdings involving corporate insiders. Transaction types are interpreted using the information and codes provided in the underlying filings.

### Does AltData US provide investment advice?
No. AltData US provides factual summaries of public regulatory disclosures. It does not provide personalized investment advice, trading recommendations, or predictions of stock prices.

### How does AltData US validate financial data?
The data processing workflow uses code-based extraction, independent parsing checks, and deterministic validation rules. Records with unresolved inconsistencies are withheld from automated publication for review.

### Does AltData US use AI to generate financial figures?
Financial figures and transaction classifications are not intended to be generated or inferred by an LLM. They are extracted from SEC disclosures and processed through deterministic application logic.

### Who operates AltData US?
AltData US is independently developed and operated.
